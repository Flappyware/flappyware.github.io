About

Flappyware LLC is a network and systems consultancy focused on the infrastructure behind AI factories and enterprise environments. We keep networks and systems running, and we help companies design, build, and operate the GPU compute, high-speed fabrics, storage, and management platforms that AI workloads depend on.

Our work spans the full lifecycle — architecture, procurement, installation coordination, system integration, bring-up, commissioning, troubleshooting, and ongoing operational support. And through a trusted development partner, we also build enterprise websites and AI-powered integrations when a project calls for software as well as infrastructure.

What we do

Network & systems administration

Day-to-day administration of the networks and systems your business runs on — reliable, secure, and well-managed, on-prem and in the cloud.

AI factory consulting

Network and systems consulting for AI factories: GPU compute, high-speed fabrics, storage, power, and cooling designed to keep training and inference running.

Enterprise networking

High-speed, low-latency fabrics: BGP, EVPN/VXLAN, InfiniBand, RoCE, and high-speed Ethernet for management and compute networks.

Data center delivery

Architecture, deployment, and commissioning of compute, storage, power, and direct liquid-cooling infrastructure — from design through bring-up.

Software & AI integration

Enterprise websites and custom software with AI built in, delivered through a trusted development partner.

Advisory & fractional leadership

IT strategy, vendor management, procurement, and fractional infrastructure leadership for teams that need senior expertise on demand.

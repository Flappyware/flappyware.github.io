About

Flappyware LLC provides technology consulting and technical program leadership for AI, high-performance computing, data centers, and enterprise infrastructure. We design and oversee the deployment of network hardware, compute systems, storage, management platforms, and direct liquid cooling infrastructure.

Our work spans the full lifecycle — architecture, procurement, installation coordination, system integration, bring-up, commissioning, troubleshooting, and ongoing operational support. In practical terms, we help companies design, build, troubleshoot, and operate complex technology infrastructure, especially AI data centers and GPU compute environments.

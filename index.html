Meet the team

Flappyware brings together data center deployment specialists, senior software developers and systems administrators around each project.

We began by developing software to automate IT, infrastructure and business processes alongside network and systems administration. Today our focus includes AI and HPC networking, deployment and operations, as well as enterprise infrastructure.

Owner and network and systems architect Brandon Jank has more than 20 years of experience across networks, systems and data centers. His background includes municipal infrastructure and university IT, where he developed PowerShell automation and an Azure-based incident response platform, followed by architecture and hands-on deployment work for high-density AI systems.

Brandon holds the NVIDIA-Certified Associate: AI Infrastructure and Operations credential. References are available on request.

Complete systems

GPU infrastructure depends on compute, network fabrics, storage, power and liquid cooling working together. Flappyware helps teams plan those interfaces and carry the design into deployment.

Areas of support

  • GPU cluster architecture, rack layouts and infrastructure planning
  • Equipment specifications, procurement and installation coordination
  • Fabric integration, system bring-up and commissioning support
  • Power and cooling integration, troubleshooting and operational support

Where we can help

Engage Flappyware for a particular phase or bring us in across the project. Deliverables are agreed around your environment.

Architecture & planning

Work through compute, network, rack, A/B power and cooling requirements. Depending on scope, deliverables can include rack layouts, connection diagrams and equipment specifications.

Deployment readiness

Review bills of materials, port maps, cabling and installation plans with your team before equipment arrives.

On-site delivery

Coordinate installation, cabling, liquid-cooling interfaces and system bring-up with engineers, technicians and equipment vendors.

A B300 deployment

A direct-liquid-cooled NVIDIA HGX B300 deployment brought compute, networking, power and cooling together in a high-density facility. Flappyware led architecture and on-site deployment work, coordinating engineers, contractors and equipment vendors.

Design the system

Developed rack layouts and network architecture for XDR InfiniBand, 400G Ethernet and out-of-band management. Coordinated power distribution and liquid-cooling integration.

Prepare the build

Produced hardware specifications, bills of materials, connection diagrams and deployment documentation. Reviewed vendor proposals for gaps and substitutions.

Support delivery

Coordinated installation, cabling, system bring-up and commissioning. Worked through hardware, optics and firmware issues alongside the deployment team.

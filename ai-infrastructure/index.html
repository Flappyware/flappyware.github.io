AI infrastructure

A B300 deployment

A direct-liquid-cooled NVIDIA HGX B300 project required compute, networks, power and cooling to work together in a real facility. Flappyware led architecture and on-site deployment work, coordinating technical decisions with engineers, contractors and equipment vendors.

Design the system

Developed rack layouts and network architecture spanning InfiniBand, Ethernet and out-of-band management, alongside power and liquid-cooling integration.

Prepare the build

Produced hardware specifications, bills of materials, connection diagrams and deployment documentation; reviewed vendor proposals for gaps and substitutions.

Support delivery

Coordinated installation, cabling, system bring-up and commissioning, helping teams work through hardware, optics and firmware issues.

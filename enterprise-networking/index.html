Networks that work

From enterprise connectivity to high-speed compute fabrics, Flappyware helps teams plan and operate networks that support demanding infrastructure.

Whether you are planning a new environment or supporting an existing one, the scope is shaped around your team and priorities.

Areas of support

  • Network architecture and design
  • BGP and EVPN/VXLAN
  • InfiniBand, RoCE and high-speed Ethernet
  • Configuration, troubleshooting and operations

Network delivery

Good network design has to survive installation, commissioning and day-to-day operations.

AI & HPC fabrics

Plan InfiniBand and Ethernet fabrics, management connectivity, port maps and cabling for GPU and storage environments.

Enterprise routing

Design and support routed networks using BGP, VRFs and Ethernet switching, with attention to addressing, segmentation and operations.

Build-ready documentation

Develop topology diagrams, rack elevations, cable schedules and handoff material to help teams install, troubleshoot and operate the network.

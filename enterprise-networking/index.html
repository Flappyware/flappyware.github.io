Networks that work

From GPU fabrics to multi-site enterprise networks, Flappyware designs and supports the routing, switching and management infrastructure that connects critical systems.

Bring us in for a design review, a deployment or a specific problem in an existing network.

Areas of support

  • 100G and 400G spine-leaf architecture
  • BGP multihoming, VRFs and EVPN/VXLAN
  • InfiniBand, RoCE and high-speed Ethernet fabrics
  • IPv6, NAT64/DNS64, troubleshooting and operations

Where we can help

From a focused design review to hands-on deployment support, the scope and deliverables depend on your environment.

Fabric design & review

Plan or review InfiniBand and Ethernet fabrics for GPU and storage environments, including management connectivity, port maps, optics and cabling.

Enterprise network work

Design routed networks using BGP, VRFs and Ethernet switching. Work through multihoming, IPv6 addressing, NAT64/DNS64 and segmentation as needed.

Deployment documentation

Produce topology diagrams, rack elevations, cable schedules and handoff material so teams can install, troubleshoot and operate the network.
